Last summer, I was fortunate to be able to take a two-day short course with [Abel Rodriguez](https://users.soe.ucsc.edu/~abel/website/Home/Home.html) here at UCSC. He based the course on the quarter-long [Art of Data Visualization](https://courses.soe.ucsc.edu/courses/ams80b/Winter15/01) course that he had taught that winter quarter. It was super interesting, and it motivated me to try a few things on my own, including a word cloud. I use [Google Voice](https://www.google.com/voice), which saves all of my text messages in spreadsheet format. I downloaded the information that I needed, and I set off. 

I decided to make one for the text messages with my wife, and another one for a friend of mine that also took the short course with me. They came out as follows:
