Every year, the first-year Applied Math and Statistics graduate students take a grueling test to show how much they have learned in the six classes they took in their first three quarters. The test consists of a four-hour written exam, immediately followed by a 48-hour take-home exam. 

I cannot access my copy of the written version of the exam, as that is not returned to us. The exam was something like taking half=sized final exams for six courses back-to-back. I don't even have any notes on problems or mistakes with my take-home exam. All we receive as feedback is at which level we passed the exam: Fail, Master's Pass, and PhD Pass.
