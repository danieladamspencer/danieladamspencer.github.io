## Cost

Living on a college campus exposes me daily to people who are dealing with the cost of college. I have seen or heard of several protests due to the recent tuition hikes after a five-year tuition freeze. Currently, the [tuition at UCSC](http://financialaid.ucsc.edu/costs/index.html) comes to $13,515 for undergraduates and $14,049 for graduates. Is the cost worth it?

According to the [Bureau of Labor Statistics](http://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2015/median-weekly-earnings-by-education-gender-race-and-ethnicity-in-2014.htm), in 2014 the median income difference between high school graduates and college graduates with an undergraduate degree was $433 per week.
