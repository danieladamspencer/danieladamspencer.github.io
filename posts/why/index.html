Why?

I've been thinking about making a personal website for a few months now. My motivations are probably a mixture of being impressed with some of my friends personal webpages, being told by podcast advertisements that a web presence is essential to life, and seeing an article or two telling me that it's the best thing since sliced bread. Plus, it would be a productive hobby, and a great place to showcase some projects that I'm working on.
